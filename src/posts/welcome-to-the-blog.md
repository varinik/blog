This is the first post on a fresh Eleventy-powered blog. It's deliberately
plain right now — soft colors, a readable layout, and nothing else — so
that a theme can be layered on top later without a rewrite.

A few things worth knowing:

- Every post lives in `src/posts/` as a markdown file.
- The homepage lists posts automatically, newest first.
- Colors, fonts, and spacing all come from `src/css/theme.css`, so
  restyling the site is mostly editing one file.

Feel free to delete this post once you've got real content in place.
