Research notes written for an audience tend to read very differently from
the ones scribbled in a private notebook. The private version is faster to
write, but the public version is usually more useful — it forces you to
say what you actually mean.

A few loose principles for this blog:

1. **Short is fine.** A post can be three paragraphs long if that's all
   there is to say.
2. **Unfinished thoughts are allowed.** Not everything needs a tidy
   conclusion.
3. **Plain language over jargon.** If a term needs a definition, define
   it inline rather than assuming the reader already knows it.

> The goal isn't to perform expertise — it's to leave a trail that's
> useful to come back to later.

More on this as the blog grows.
